Third-party software included with VPadConnect
================================================

ViGEmBus 1.22.0
---------------

The VPadConnect installer contains the original, unmodified and signed
ViGEmBus 1.22.0 installer from Nefarius Software Solutions e.U. ViGEmBus is a
separate system-level driver and is not part of VPadConnect. VPadConnect is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Nefarius Software Solutions e.U.

ViGEmBus was retired and its repository archived on November 2, 2023. Version
1.22.0 is its final release and will not receive upstream updates. Compatibility
with future Windows releases is not guaranteed.
